# Ticketz Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand Identity

### Brand Name

**Ticketz**

The brand name must always be written exactly as:

**Ticketz**

Do not use:

* TICKETZ
* ticketz
* TicketZ
* Ticket-Z
* Ticket z

Always preserve the capitalization:

**T + icketz**

---

## 2. Brand Concept

Ticketz is a modern cinema price comparison platform.

The visual identity should communicate:

* Modern
* Classy
* Reliable
* Practical
* Premium without feeling luxurious or inaccessible
* Clean
* Trustworthy

The interface should feel appropriate for a cinema-related product while remaining functional as a price comparison tool.

Avoid making the website look like:

* A generic movie streaming platform
* A childish entertainment website
* A discount marketplace
* An overly corporate banking application
* A cluttered ticket-booking website

---

## 3. Primary Color Palette

Use these three colors as the primary brand colors.

### Deep Burgundy

**#8B0000**

Primary brand/accent color.

Recommended uses:

* Primary buttons
* Important actions
* Selected states
* Key highlights
* Brand accents
* Important price/comparison indicators

Use it intentionally rather than covering the entire interface in burgundy.

---

### Warm Ivory

**#F5F5DC**

Primary light/background color.

Recommended uses:

* Main background
* Cards
* Large UI surfaces
* Empty space
* Secondary sections

This color should contribute to the classy and warm character of the brand.

---

### Deep Navy

**#1A2954**

Secondary dark brand color.

Recommended uses:

* Headings
* Navigation
* Secondary buttons
* Text accents
* Footer
* Dark UI sections

---

## 4. Color Balance

The three colors should work together rather than being used in equal proportions.

Preferred hierarchy:

* #F5F5DC — dominant background/light surface
* #1A2954 — primary dark structural color
* #8B0000 — selective accent/action color

Avoid excessive use of #8B0000.

The burgundy should feel intentional and premium rather than visually overwhelming.

---

## 5. Typography

Typography should be:

* Clean
* Modern
* Highly readable
* Slightly sophisticated

Use a contemporary sans-serif typeface for most UI text.

Typography hierarchy should be obvious:

* Large, strong heading
* Clear section heading
* Readable body text
* Compact supporting information

Avoid overly decorative fonts.

If a serif typeface is introduced, use it sparingly as an accent rather than as the primary UI font.

---

## 6. Layout

Use a clean modern layout with:

* Generous whitespace
* Clear visual hierarchy
* Consistent spacing
* Rounded but restrained UI elements
* Strong alignment
* Clear comparison cards/tables

Do not overcrowd the interface.

The primary user action — comparing cinema prices — should remain visually dominant.

---

## 7. Cinema Comparison UI

The comparison interface is the most important component of Ticketz.

Each cinema should have a clearly identifiable comparison card or table row.

Important information should be visually prioritized:

1. Cinema name
2. Location
3. Selected date
4. Ticket price
5. Price difference
6. Data source / last updated time

The cheapest valid option should be visually emphasized.

Example visual hierarchy:

**XXI DP Mall**

Rp35.000

**Cheapest**

while more expensive alternatives remain clearly visible but less prominent.

Do not use visual emphasis that makes users believe an unverified price is cheaper.

---

## 8. Buttons and Interactive Elements

Primary actions should use:

**#8B0000**

with sufficient contrast.

Secondary actions may use:

**#1A2954**

Buttons should have:

* Clear labels
* Consistent sizing
* Comfortable touch targets
* Subtle hover/focus states

Avoid excessive button styles.

---

## 9. Cards

Cards should have a modern, restrained appearance.

Recommended characteristics:

* Warm ivory or white-like surface
* Subtle border or shadow
* Moderate corner radius
* Generous internal spacing

Avoid:

* Excessive shadows
* Excessive gradients
* Glassmorphism as the dominant design language
* Excessively rounded "bubble" interfaces

The overall appearance should remain classy and mature.

---

## 10. Icons and Imagery

Use simple, modern icons.

Icons should support usability rather than becoming decorative clutter.

Cinema/movie imagery can be used where appropriate, but the interface should not depend heavily on large movie posters.

The primary product value is **price comparison**, not movie discovery.

---

## 11. Brand Voice

Ticketz should communicate in a tone that is:

* Concise
* Helpful
* Clear
* Confident
* Practical

Avoid overly promotional language.

Prefer:

"Compare ticket prices before you go."

over:

"Experience the ultimate cinema adventure with Ticketz!"

The product should feel like a useful tool, not an advertisement.

---

## 12. Data Transparency

Because Ticketz compares prices, trust is a critical part of the brand experience.

Whenever possible, clearly communicate:

* Where the price came from
* When the price was last checked
* Whether the price is estimated or verified
* Whether a price may have changed

Transparency should be integrated into the UI without overwhelming the user.

---

## 13. Responsive Design

The brand experience must remain consistent across:

* Desktop
* Tablet
* Mobile

On mobile, prioritize:

1. Cinema selection
2. Date selection
3. Price comparison
4. Cheapest option
5. Source/update information

Do not simply shrink the desktop interface.

Adapt the comparison layout appropriately for smaller screens.

---

## 14. Logo and Wordmark

The primary written brand representation is:

**Ticketz**

Do not alter its capitalization.

If a logo is created, it should reflect the same visual identity:

* Modern
* Classy
* Cinema-related without being overly literal
* Simple enough to work at small sizes

Avoid generic popcorn/cinema-ticket clichés unless they are incorporated in a distinctive and restrained way.

---

## 15. Non-Negotiable Brand Rules

1. Always write the brand name as **Ticketz**.
2. Never use all-uppercase or all-lowercase versions of the brand name.
3. Use **#8B0000**, **#F5F5DC**, and **#1A2954** as the core brand palette.
4. Maintain a modern and classy visual direction.
5. Prioritize readability and usability over decoration.
6. Keep the cinema price comparison as the visual and functional centerpiece.
7. Do not introduce colors that significantly conflict with the primary palette.
8. Do not make the interface visually childish or excessively playful.
9. Maintain consistent spacing, typography, buttons, cards, and interaction patterns throughout the application.
10. The visual design must support trust and transparency because Ticketz displays price information that users may rely on.
